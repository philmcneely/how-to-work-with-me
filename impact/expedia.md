# Expedia Group: Quality Engineering for Lodging

**Senior Engineering Manager, Operational Excellence** — Jul 2020 to Sep 2022

## The Role

Quality engineering focal for the Lodging organization — the business unit that includes Vrbo, the platform I'd spent 13 years building testing for at HomeAway. Different company, same product, much larger scale.

## What I Did

Influenced product and engineering roadmaps by leading teams that consulted on quality engineering practices across Lodging. The work was about convergence — getting dozens of teams aligned on how, where, and what to automate, how to write and design tests, and how to move toward continuous testing.

Managed and developed a distributed team of engineers and testers. Promoted a continuous learning environment through hands-on learning, educational opportunities, and a community of practice.

Built strategic partnerships across the Expedia Group brand and verticals to drive adoption of quality best practices.

## Tools and Infrastructure

JIRA, Confluence, IntelliJ, Groovy, Jenkins, AWS, Artifactory, Docker, Node.js, GitHub Actions, WebDriverIO, NightwatchJS.
