# FullStory: Engineering Velocity Through Quality Infrastructure

As Director of Engineering at FullStory (2022-2025), I owned the product and technical roadmap for internal tooling platforms and developer productivity. Drove cultural transformation toward automation-first and risk-aware quality practices across product teams.

## Measurable Outcomes

| Metric | Before | After | Impact |
|--------|--------|-------|--------|
| CI build pass rate | ~52% | ~77% | Developers stopped ignoring red builds |
| CI vendor costs | Baseline | -50%+ | Moved runners to our GKE/GCE clusters — discounted compute rates instead of paying CircleCI for runtime |
| Team size | 11 engineers | 4 engineers | Company-wide RIF and restructuring — trimmed scope, supported those who left, remaining team still delivered |
| Median PR build time | Baseline | -32% | Selective test execution optimization |
| Release verification | 2.5+ hour dev/test final verification, frequent last-minute blockers | 15-20 min automated suite | Comprehensive CI caught issues early — no more surprise blockers at release |

## What I Built

FullStory went through multiple rounds of RIFs and reorgs while I was there — the company kept pivoting how it did things. I navigated through all of them except the last. Each round reduced the team further, from 11 down to 4. What was my choice was how to respond. I refocused on core strengths, efficiency, cost, and speed to deliver. Cost and velocity were the drumbeat — every decision ran through both. We moved CI runners from CircleCI's infrastructure to our own GKE/GCE clusters — same orchestration, but our discounted compute rates instead of paying CircleCI for runtime. Eliminated redundant tooling and optimized compute spend across the board. Developer and test final verification used to take at least 2.5 hours — sometimes longer — and frequently turned up last-minute blockers because CI wasn't catching things early enough. We built comprehensive automated suites that caught issues in the pipeline, not at the gate. Verification dropped to 15-20 minutes. We ran it as platform-as-a-service — still looking for improvements, optimizing, adding features, just at a slower pace with fewer people. The framework was open for other teams to contribute if they wanted to, but in practice my team continued owning and driving the work.

- Co-defined an executive-facing Quality/CI/DevEx scorecard — owned several metrics on it and was responsible for keeping them green. Also maintained an internal team scorecard
- Pioneered AI-driven testing accelerators — led evaluations, authored enablement content, conducted hands-on workshops
- Directed platform engineering for internal search systems, including management of multiple Solr clusters and autocomplete functionality
- Oversaw internal tooling platform supporting local development and CI/CD pipelines within a monorepo architecture
- Delivered scalable, developer-friendly infrastructure using CircleCI, GitHub, and in-house telemetry solutions
