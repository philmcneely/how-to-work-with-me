# References

Resources that inspired this portfolio, and good examples if you're thinking about building your own.

## Manager README Collections

- [Manager READMEs](https://svnk.github.io/manager-READMEs/) — Curated list of manager READMEs from engineering leaders. Good starting point to see the range of approaches.
- [12 Manager READMEs from Silicon Valley's Top Tech Companies](https://hackernoon.com/12-manager-readmes-from-silicon-valleys-top-tech-companies-26588a660afe) — Examples from Slack, HubSpot, Netflix, Etsy, Shopify, and others.

## Individual Examples Worth Reading

- [Lara Hogan](https://larahogan.me/management/) — Former VP Engineering at Kickstarter, Engineering Director at Etsy. Her [first 1:1 questions](https://larahogan.me/blog/first-one-on-one-questions/) and [Resilient Management](https://resilient-management.com/) book are essential reading for anyone managing people.
- [Molly White](https://github.com/molly/manager-README) — Tech lead README. Short, direct, well-structured.
- [Katie Wilde](https://medium.com/@kawomersley/why-and-how-to-share-your-manager-readme-plus-heres-mine-8a4fe188ee1b) — Includes her own README plus a good writeup on why and how to share one.

## On the Practice Itself

- [Avoiding Mistakes with your Manager README](https://mattnewkirk.com/2019/01/02/avoiding-mistakes-with-your-manager-readme/) — Honest look at what goes wrong when these become performative instead of useful.
