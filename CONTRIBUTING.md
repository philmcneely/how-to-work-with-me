# Contributing

This is a personal leadership portfolio, not a collaborative project. But feedback is welcome — if something reads wrong, doesn't match your experience working with me, or could be stronger, I want to hear it.

## What's useful

- Specific feedback on a section — quote the line, say what's off, suggest what would be better.
- Questions about something that's unclear or seems contradictory.
- Calling out something that sounds good on paper but doesn't survive contact with reality.

## What's not useful

- General opinions about leadership philosophy. This is how I work, not a debate.
- Style preferences. I wrote it this way on purpose.
- Requests to add content about topics I haven't covered. If it's not here, there's usually a reason.

## How to submit

Use the [issue templates](https://github.com/philmcneely/how-to-work-with-me/issues/new/choose). Blank issues are disabled. Pick Feedback or Question and fill in the fields.
